Inequality of income distribution S80/S20 (income quintile share ratio)


Definition:

Ratio of total income received by the 20% of the population with the highest income (top quintile) to that received by the 20% of the population with the lowest income (lowest quintile).


Contact person on methodology:
GUS – Departament Badań Społecznych
