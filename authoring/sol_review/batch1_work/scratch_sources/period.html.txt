Use a period at the end of a complete sentence that is a statement. Example: I know him well. Rule 2. If the last item in the sentence is an abbreviation that ends in a period, do not follow it with another period. Incorrect: This is Alice Smith, M.D.. Correct: This is Alice Smith, M.D. Correct: Please shop, cook, etc. We will do the laundry. Rule 3. Question marks and exclamation points replace and eliminate periods at the end of a sentence.
